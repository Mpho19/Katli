26 Years
& Counting

Paballo has known Katli for almost her whole life. Here is what she wants her to hear on this birthday.

Happy Birthday, Katli! ❤️🎂🥹

I’ve known you for 26 years of my life, and what a beautiful 26 years it has been! We’ve had the privilege of witnessing each other’s milestones, celebrating each other’s victories, and growing through different seasons of life. And the beautiful thing is that we still have so many more milestones to witness and memories to create together. ❤️

I know Ausi Jane is no longer here with us, and I can only imagine how much her presence is missed, especially on days like these. But I want you to always remember that you are never alone. We are here for you, we love you, and we will continue to stand by you through every chapter of your life.

06
“You were made for more.”

As you step into this new chapter, may it bring you endless joy, peace, love, and happiness. May God grant you the desires of your heart and guide your every step. My prayer for your chapter 28 is that you learn to choose yourself, unapologetically. Let go of anything that no longer serves you, anything that hinders your growth, and anything that steals your peace. Choose the things and people that bring you genuine happiness, love, and fulfilment. Never forget that you are surrounded by so much love, and there is still so much greatness ahead of you. You were made for more, and I pray that you go further than you have ever imagined. 🙏🏽❤️

And lastly, thank you for giving me my firstborn, LOL! 😭😂❤️ You are such an amazing mother, and I pray that God continues to give you the wisdom, patience, grace, and strength you need to become the very best mother you can be to Omphile. May He guide you as you raise her and bless the beautiful bond you share.

07

I love you so much, Katli. May God continue to cover you, protect you, bless you, and keep you beautiful inside and out. May this new chapter be everything your heart has been praying for and so much more.

Happy birthday, my girl! Stay blessed, stay beautiful, and never forget how deeply you are loved. ❤️🥂🎉

— Paballo

Her gossip partner, her forever person. Kele on the bond that only they understand.

My big sister, my forever person, my gossip partner. I don’t think I tell you enough how much you mean to me, but I hope you know that having you as my sister is one of the greatest blessings in my life. Thank you for being part of my life, for the love you give me. One thing I’ll always cherish about us is the silly moments, the laughter, the jokes nobody else understands, and all the little things that make our relationship ours. Those are the memories I hope we’ll still be laughing about years from now.

10

I know we may not always express our love for each other perfectly, but nothing will ever change the fact that you’re my sister, and I will always love you. I pray that God continues to protect you, guide you, bless you, and give you everything your heart desires because you deserve so much happiness, peace, love, and success. I hope life is gentle with you, even on the days when things get hard, and I hope you never forget how special, valuable, and loved you are.

“You mean more to me than you’ll ever truly know.”

No matter how old we get or where life takes us, you’ll always be my big sister, and I’ll always be grateful that God chose you to be part of my life. I love you more than words can explain, and I’ll always be rooting for you. May we continue to grow together, make beautiful memories, and have a bond that only gets stronger with time. You mean more to me than you’ll ever truly know.

— Kele

In her own words

Dear
Future Me ❤️✨

A letter Katli wrote to the woman she will be in 2036.

Happy birthday to me! 🥂🎂❤️

Wow, ten years later! I wonder what kind of woman you have become, what you have achieved, and how much your life has changed since the day I wrote this letter.

The past years have not been easy. I have faced pain, loss, and moments when I felt lost, especially this year after losing my uncle, one of my pillars of strength. My heart has been heavy, but I believe that with time, I will heal. I hope that today, ten years later, I have found peace and learnt to carry my memories with love rather than pain.

16

I am deeply grateful for my siblings and for Ausi Mathabo, Abuti Mohau, Abuti Thabang, and Abuti Kamohelo. Thank you for being my strength when I didn’t know where to turn for support. Thank you for standing by me during moments when life felt overwhelming. I pray that life blesses you all abundantly, because your love and support mean more than words can express. ❤️🙏

Dear future me, I hope you have made it. I hope you are successful, financially independent, happy, and at peace with yourself. I hope you turned your dreams into reality and became the woman you once prayed to be. May you never forget the difficult days that shaped you or the strength it took to keep going.

Most importantly, I hope my beautiful daughter, who was only ten when I wrote this letter, is now a happy, healthy, confident twenty-year-old. I hope I have given her a beautiful life, made her proud, and shown her that no matter how difficult life becomes, we must never give up on our dreams.

17

I pray that these ten years have brought healing, growth, beautiful opportunities, genuine love, and blessings beyond my imagination. I hope I have learnt to love myself, protect my peace, appreciate my journey, and celebrate even the smallest victories.

And if life hasn’t turned out exactly as I hoped, I pray I have the courage to keep going.

“My journey doesn’t have to be perfect to be beautiful.”

To the woman reading this in 2036: Never forget the woman who wrote these words. She was hurting, she was healing, she was uncertain about the future, but she still dared to dream. She believed in you before she knew how everything would turn out.

I hope you made her proud. ❤️

18

Happy birthday to me! Here’s to a decade of growth, healing, success, and becoming everything I once dreamed of being.

I am proud of the woman I was, grateful for the woman I am, and excited about the woman I am becoming.

With love, me… ten years ago, believing in a better tomorrow. ❤️✨

— Katli
